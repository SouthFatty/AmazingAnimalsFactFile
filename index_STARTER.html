Fennec Fox

About My Animal

Fennec foxes, also known as Vulpes zerda, are the smallest canine species in the world.
They live in the Sahara Desert and North Africa.
They are also nocturnal predators, used to extreme heat and little water.

Fun Facts:

  • Fennec foxes stand only around 8 inches tall.
  • Fully grown adults usually weigh from 2 to 3.5 pounds.
  • They have a thick, cream-colored coat that helps them maintain their body temperature.

Routine:

  1. Morning- As the sun rises, they go into their underground burrows.
  2. Midday- Fennec foxes spend the hottest parts of the day sleeping in their burrows.
  3. Afternoon- They spend time with their family communities of up to 10 individuals, playing and grooming each other.
  4. Dusk- Now, Fennec foxes come out from their burrows.
  5. Night- They hunt during the night, being nocturnal predators.
  6. Dawn- The next day starts with them retreating back into their burrows.
